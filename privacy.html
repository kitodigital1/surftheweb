Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SurfTheWeb handles information when you visit and use the website.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Information we collect

SurfTheWeb is designed to function without requiring visitors to create an account or provide personal information.

The website may nevertheless process limited technical information automatically when you visit, such as information normally transmitted by a web browser when requesting a website.

Local storage

SurfTheWeb may use your browser's local storage to remember recently visited destinations. This helps prevent the Surf button from repeatedly sending you to the same websites.

Information stored by SurfTheWeb in your browser's local storage remains on your device unless you clear it or otherwise remove it through your browser.

Cookies and advertising

SurfTheWeb may display advertising provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to their own policies and applicable law.

Where required by applicable law, SurfTheWeb may provide visitors with choices regarding cookies, personalized advertising, and related data processing through an appropriate consent or privacy-management mechanism.

Third-party websites

SurfTheWeb contains links to websites operated by third parties. When you follow one of these links, the third party may collect information about you according to its own privacy practices.

SurfTheWeb is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or data-processing activities of external websites.

Analytics and technical services

SurfTheWeb may use third-party services in the future to understand website traffic, maintain the service, prevent abuse, or improve the user experience.

Any such services may collect technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

SurfTheWeb is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because SurfTheWeb links to external websites, visitors should also consider the age-appropriateness and privacy practices of any third-party destination they choose to visit.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as SurfTheWeb changes, new services are introduced, or legal and regulatory requirements change.

The date shown at the beginning of this policy indicates when it was most recently updated.

Contact

If you have a privacy-related question or request, please use the contact method provided by SurfTheWeb.
