About SurfTheWeb

SurfTheWeb is a simple way to explore the internet without knowing exactly where you're going.

What is SurfTheWeb?

The internet is enormous, and much of it is easy to overlook. SurfTheWeb was created around a simple idea: press a button and see where the web takes you.

Each time you surf, SurfTheWeb selects a website from our curated collection of destinations. The collection includes educational resources, interesting projects, communities, archives, unusual websites, historical material, games, experiments, and other corners of the public internet.

What you'll find

SurfTheWeb is intentionally varied. Some destinations are informative. Some are strange. Some are obscure. Some may be unsettling or mysterious.

We attempt to keep the normal discovery pool appropriate for a general audience and ad-supported environment. Websites included in the collection may nevertheless have their own content, policies, and audience restrictions.

Third-party websites

SurfTheWeb does not own or control the websites that appear through the Surf feature.

When you leave SurfTheWeb, you are visiting a third-party website. That website may collect information, use cookies, display advertising, or provide content according to its own policies.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies and terms of any external website they choose to visit.

Our goal

SurfTheWeb is built around curiosity and discovery. There is no destination you have to search for and no particular algorithmic feed you need to follow.

Just surf.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or website suggestions can be directed through the contact method provided on SurfTheWeb.
