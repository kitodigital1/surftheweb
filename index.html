What is SurfTheWeb?

SurfTheWeb is a random web discovery experience. Instead of searching for something specific, press SURF and see where the internet takes you.

Our collection includes interesting, educational, creative, obscure, strange, and mysterious websites from across the public web.

Each surf randomly selects a destination from our curated collection. Recently visited websites are remembered in your browser so you are less likely to immediately encounter the same destination again.
